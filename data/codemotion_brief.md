# Codemotion Madrid 2026 — Brief

## What it is

Codemotion Madrid is a tech conference for the developer community. It brings
together developers, tech leaders, and industry practitioners for two days of
talks, workshops, and networking.

## When and where

- **Dates:** April 20–21, 2026
- **Venue:** La Nave, Madrid (Spain)

## Audience

Developers, tech leaders, and students. The conference is aimed at working
practitioners and people actively building software.

## Format

- **Talks:** Conference sessions across the ten tracks.
- **Workshops:** Hands-on sessions (advance registration required).
- **Exhibition area:** Sponsors, companies, and community booths.
- **Networking:** Structured networking opportunities throughout the event,
  including a dedicated "Refresh & Reload" networking moment.

## Story and timeline

Codemotion started in 2006, when co-founders Chiara Russo and Mara Marzocchi
organized a tech event called JavaDay in Rome, driven by their love for
technology. Over the years it evolved from an events organizer into a broader
ecosystem connecting developers and companies year-round.

- **2006** — Origins: Chiara Russo and Mara Marzocchi organize JavaDay in Rome.
- **2013** — Codemotion transitions from hobby to startup, with the founders
  working on it full-time.
- **2017** — The developer community grows significantly; Codemotion raises a
  €1.5M investment round to improve the developer experience.
- **2020** — €6M funding round with P101, Primomiglio, and CDP Venture Capital
  SGR, accelerating platform development.
- **2021** — Shift fully online; leadership strengthened, with a renewed focus
  on recruiting solutions for companies.
- **2022** — Launch of Codemotion Talent, a platform matching developers
  looking for jobs with companies hiring technical staff.
- **2023** — €8M investment round, targeting international expansion, product
  development, and team growth.
